How to use

  • Draw: tap to add a point. It goes into the line where it fits best.
  • Drag any point to move it. Tap one to select it, then Delete.
  • Move: drag to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Turn the map: hold the mouse wheel down and drag sideways, or drag the round handle bottom right. Double-click the handle to straighten.
  • Red parts of your line leave the tarmac.
  • Chevrons show the driving direction. Use Reverse if they point the wrong way.
  • Save, then tell Claude which track: it becomes the autopilot's line and gets a test drive.

Drivers

In GT4: give AI cars 1–5 drivers and press Send to AI cars. GT4 shows their names from the next race in every race: the timing tower set to Driver names, the telemetry and phone pages, and the launcher's Race Lab tab (AI car 1 is the first car in the race that isn't yours, and so on). GT4's own AI still drives every car, so the six settings don't change how they drive yet.

Give a car a driver and it drives with that driver's personality; change a driver here and every car they drive follows. Pace is the share of the line's speeds they aim for. Speed and Braking change their pace and braking points a little every lap (how consistent they are); Mistakes adds late braking, missed apexes and early throttle; Passing sets how hard they attack and defend; Contact sets how much room they leave other cars (careful leaves the most).
